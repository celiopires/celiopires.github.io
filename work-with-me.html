Frequently asked questions

What does a design system engagement with you look like?

It usually starts with an audit: what exists in Figma, what exists in code, and where the two have drifted apart. From there we agree on one source of truth for tokens, a component set that matches what actually ships, written rules for when to use what, and a plan to drive adoption across teams.


Can you design an MCP connector or AI agent experience for my product?

Yes. I designed and built a live MCP connector for Ensinova that lets school owners run their business from Claude or ChatGPT, with safety enforced by the server rather than by the prompt. I can help you decide what your product should expose to AI assistants, design the conversational flows, and make sure risky actions stay under control.


How do I make my design system usable by AI coding agents?

Agents only know what is written down. Keep one machine-readable source of truth for tokens, give agents your real components with their actual props and variants instead of lookalikes, and write down the rules a senior designer carries in their head: responsive behaviour, contrast requirements, copy and tone. Then let a governance agent check every change against those rules.
